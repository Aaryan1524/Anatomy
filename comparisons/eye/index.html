Two fresh agents got the same request word for word: "Explain how the human eye works, as one interactive figure." One followed SKILL.md from main, the other the literal-first version. Neither was told what kind of object to build or that the two would be compared. Pick a scenario below to play it in both figures at once, answer the question for yourself, then reveal the answer.

What the test found: both skills built the real eye; neither reached for a camera or another analogy. The eye is a physical subject, and the old skill already drew physical subjects as themselves (like its rocket engine). The difference between the two skills shows up on abstract ideas, such as the perceptron, which the old skill turned into a beam balance. So these two figures differ in craft and detail, not in kind.
